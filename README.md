CapeVerse 🌍

From Cape Town to the World.

CapeVerse is a South African-born social networking and digital community platform developed by TCprojects.

Created with the ambition of connecting people through digital interaction, content sharing, personal profiles and private communication, CapeVerse represents an independent technology initiative originating in Cape Town, South Africa.

«Currently in closed alpha development. CapeVerse has not officially launched, and no public launch date has been announced.»

---

Project Overview

CapeVerse is being developed as a social platform where people can build their digital presence, share content, connect with others and participate in online communities.

The platform's development follows a phased approach, beginning with core social functionality and expanding into messaging, notifications, search and discovery.

The project is being developed and refined through controlled testing, feature improvements and ongoing feedback.

Architecture Overview

CapeVerse uses a web application architecture supported by backend services and a relational database.

At a high level, the platform consists of:

1. A user-facing social networking interface.
2. Application functionality for profiles, content, search and messaging.
3. Backend services for authentication and real-time interactions.
4. Database services for application information.
5. Access-control mechanisms to protect user data.

This repository documents the product and its development. It does not contain the private application implementation.
